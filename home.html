Seungho Shin

Currently, I am a research intern at the Machine Learning and Intelligence Lab (MLILAB) at KAIST. Before joining KAIST, I received my Master’s degree from the Visual Artificial Intelligence Lab (VAILAB) at Kyung Hee University in 2025, advised by Prof. Jung Uk Kim. I also worked with Prof. Gyeong-Moon Park through collaborative research. I received my B.S. in Software Convergence from Kyung Hee University in 2024.

My work aims to address key challenges in multimodal learning and data-centric AI. Specifically, I focus on building user-friendly and real-world applicable multimodal systems.
